# SF Camera Auto Align - Quickstart Example

---

1. Add **Camera Auto Align** to `BP_ThirdPersonCharacter`.
2. Set `AutoAlignDelay = 2`, `AlignInterpolationSpeed = 3`, `AlignmentMode = AlignToMovementDirection`.
3. While aiming: `Set Auto Align Enabled (false)`; on release `Set Auto Align Enabled (true)`.

> 💡 Open `Levels/HowToUse` in the plugin content to compare the modes in play mode.
